# Bug sweep and polish pass, November 2026

The last work before this pass was on 2026-10-08. This pass covered the smallbiz edition only; `pro/` was left alone.

## How it was tested

- Played the game with Playwright against `npm run dev`. Covered all three packs (dental, manufacturer, ecommerce), every chip, manual picks, Clear, Skip, a full four-quarter game to the report, all three replay buttons, Copy link and the share picture.
- Edge inputs:
  - Share code field: empty, spaces, emoji, 5,000 characters, markup.
  - URL `b`: unknown pack, upper case.
  - URL `s`: 7-character base36 codes.
  - Playtest params `fix` and `auto` with unknown ids.
- Ran each screen at a 375 px viewport: checked touch target sizes and horizontal overflow.
- Played with the keyboard only, then emulated reduced motion and forced colors (Windows High Contrast).
- Measured contrast of the focus rings.

No console errors apart from the favicon 404. No horizontal overflow at 375 px.

## Findings, ranked

| # | Area | Finding | Status |
|---|------|---------|--------|
| 1 | Logic | **Reveal Skip did not stop the reveal timers.** After Skip, the pending timers kept firing: the months replayed, the Next button vanished and came back, and the hit list shrank back to the count the timer had reached. | Fixed, with a test |
| 2 | A11y | **Focus fell to `<body>` when the focused control disappeared.** This happened on reveal Skip, on closing the asset panel, on removing a pick, on Clear, and on "Make the picture". Keyboard users had to start again from the top of the page. | Fixed |
| 3 | A11y | **Opening an asset left focus on its tile.** The panel came after eight more tiles and all the chips in tab order, and Esc did nothing. | Fixed: focus moves into the panel, and Esc or Close returns it to the tile |
| 4 | A11y | **The cash pile was an `aria-live` region on a number redrawn every frame**, so screen readers could hear a stream of partial dollar amounts on every screen. | Fixed: one hidden live value that only changes when the target changes |
| 5 | A11y | **Focus rings disappeared in forced-colors mode.** They were `box-shadow` with `outline: none`. The inset ring on a picked finding card measured 2.9:1 against the card. | Fixed: transparent outline fallback; darker inset ring |
| 6 | A11y | **Cards that won't fit were natively `disabled`.** That removed them from tab order, so keyboard and screen-reader users never heard "Won't fit". | Fixed: `aria-disabled`, still focusable |
| 7 | A11y | **Touch targets under 44 px at 375 px:** chips (40 px tall), small buttons (40 px), the skip link, the report's "show fixes" toggles (20 px), the footer link and the CTA's secondary link. | Fixed for chips, small buttons, the skip link and the fixes toggles (on coarse pointers). The footer and CTA links are inline links in a sentence, which WCAG 2.5.8 exempts, so they were left as they are |
| 8 | A11y | **The intro's premise section was one live region** holding the h1 and the Play button, so changing business re-read all of it, button included. | Fixed: only the tagline and premise text are live |
| 9 | Motion | **Opening a panel on a narrow screen used smooth scrolling even with reduced motion on.** Everything else respects the setting: the reveal is instant and no CSS animations run. | Fixed |
| 10 | Logic | **7-character base36 share codes above `1z141z3` (2^32 - 1) wrapped past 2^32.** `1z141z4` became seed 0, so different codes collided. | Fixed, with a test: out-of-range codes are hashed like words |
| 11 | Logic | **`?b=DENTAL` didn't match the pack id** and silently fell back to the default. | Fixed, with a test: pack ids are lowercased |
| 12 | Polish | **No favicon.** Every load logged a 404. | Fixed: inline SVG favicon |
| 13 | Logic | **The report's "Break-ins" counted a failed audit as a break-in.** It used `incidentCount`, which includes the audit incident, and the audit already has its own row. A machine-shop year with 5 break-ins and a failed questionnaire showed 6. Found in the live playtest. | Fixed, with a test: counts `exploited` incidents only. The engine's `incidentCount` is unchanged |

Verified OK, no change:
- Focus moves to each new screen's h1.
- All report buttons work.
- Copy link announces "Copied".
- The share image has alt text.
- Unknown `b`, `auto` and `fix` values fall back cleanly.
- The intro tab order is sensible.
- Contrast of text pairs is covered by `theme.test.ts`.
- The page focus ring measures 3.1:1 on paper.

## Live playtest after deploy

After the deploy, the game was played again on https://flintscope.com/game/:
- A full machine-shop year mixing manual picks, chips and Skip quarter. Cash, days closed and letters added up; Break-ins did not (row 13).
- A full year at 375 px with touch.
- A keyboard-only year with reduced motion.
- Edge links and every report button.

Checks that passed:
- Each replay button reproduces its result. "Same code" with the same picks gives the same loss. The copied link reproduces the year too.
- `?b=DENTAL` and out-of-range codes work live.
- After "Make the picture", focus moves to Save.
- After "Different business", focus moves to the intro's h1.
- No console errors.

## For you: design and gameplay notes (not changed)

1. **Unknown `?b=` falls back to dental without a word.** A shared link with a typo opens a different business. Consider a one-line notice on the intro.
2. **The asset panel sits below the map on phones.** Each tile tap scrolls the page down, and the player scrolls back up for the next tile. A bottom sheet, or opening the panel inline under the tapped row, would cut the scrolling.
3. **The Clear chip appears and disappears with the picks,** which shifts the chip row. Consider keeping it in place, disabled when there is nothing to clear.
4. **The reveal's Skip is small and sits under the map.** On a phone it is below the fold during the reveal. A Skip in the header, next to the cash pile, would be reachable.
5. **Selected state relies on fill colour.** That covers selected tiles, chips, the business picker and picked cards. In forced-colors mode the check mark on cards survives, but pressed chips and tiles look the same as unpressed ones. Consider a border or icon change for pressed state.
6. **Resolved in phase 5: `useAnimatedNumber` was removed from `skins/smallbiz/hooks.ts`.** Original note: If kept, note that interrupting it restarts from a stale value; CashPile has the corrected version.
7. **The report's luck strip and cash pile both animate or draw on load.** With seven bars below, the report is long on a phone, at about 4,000 px. A collapsed "How the other orders did" might help.

From the live playtest:

8. **The cash pile replays last quarter's loss.** Each round screen drains the pile from the previous quarter's starting cash (`cashPrev`, added on purpose in 8787785), so the player watches the same loss twice, once in the reveal and again on the next quarter. Consider starting the round pile where the reveal ended.
9. **Dave's first breach line, "Told you that one would bite.",** implies a warning that the game never gave. It reads oddly in quarter 1. Consider a first-quarter line that doesn't refer back.
10. **Skip quarter commits right away, with points unspent and no confirmation.** One stray tap gives up a whole quarter. Consider a confirm step when points are left, or an undo on the reveal.
11. **The report says "100 more times" in one line and "Across 200 years" in the next.** These are the luck replays and the long run, but side by side they look like a contradiction. Consider matching the counts, or naming the two clearly.
12. **On a phone, the reveal's break-in cards and Next sit below the fold.** The player sees the map shake, then has to scroll to learn what happened. This goes with note 4.
13. **"0/5 points" reads two ways.** It is points left out of the budget, but it can read as points spent. Consider "0 of 5 left" or "5 of 5 spent".
14. **It takes 16 Tab presses from the asset panel to Fix.** The commit bar comes after every finding card and chip. Consider a "Go to Fix" skip link, or putting the commit bar earlier in tab order.
15. **Browser Back or Reload in the middle of a year drops the game.** Back leaves the site, because the game pushes no history. Reload returns to the intro with the same code. Consider pushing a history entry per screen, or saving the year in session storage.
