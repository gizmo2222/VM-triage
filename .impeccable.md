# Design Context

Written from the FlintScope project brief, the brand guide in the flintscope repo, and the owner's direction during the build. Correct anything here that is wrong; the design skills read it first.

## Who it is for

Owners of small businesses with no security staff: a dentist, a machine-shop owner, an online-store founder. They are playing on a phone, probably from a LinkedIn post, with about five minutes. They do not know what CVE, CVSS, EPSS or KEV mean and must never see those words.

Secondary: security practitioners who will judge FlintScope's credibility by whether the game's logic is sound. They get the pro skin later.

## What it must feel like

A short, sharp game with a point, not a survey and not a lecture. The owner said the first version "felt like a Google form" and asked for more fun and less reading. Tension during the reveal, a real sting when something breaks, and a report card that makes the lesson land: you cannot fix everything, so the order matters.

Tone: dry, direct, a little wry. The IT person has a voice. No exclamation marks, no cheerleading, no fear-mongering.

## Brand

FlintScope: brass on ink. Brass #C9A24B, ink #0F1620, slate #6B7785, paper #F2F0EB. Brand typefaces per the brand guide are Spectral (serif, headings), IBM Plex Sans (body) and IBM Plex Mono (eyebrows, labels, numbers). The theme file currently names Fraunces and Source Sans 3 and loads neither; treat that as a placeholder.

The brand guide's own site is dark (ink background). The game is light (paper background) on purpose, for a phone in daylight and for a friendlier first impression than a security vendor's dark site. Keep brass as the accent, not the surface.

## Practitioner edition (`/pro/`)

Audience: security leads, vCISOs, MSSP analysts, and hiring managers judging Nick's method. Ink ground, brass accent, Plex Mono numbers, matching flintscope.com. Real terminology is required here, not merely allowed. The credibility of the whole project rides on this skin being honest: the blended formula is published with its weights, the SSVC tree says it is an approximation, and ground truth is shown after every run. Phones matter here too, because the links get tapped from LinkedIn; the table must degrade to card rows below about 700px. Owner decisions from the 2026-09-18 critique: card rows on phones, exact formula published, five methods shown with three behind More.

## Constraints

- Mobile-first, keyboard playable, nothing carried by colour alone, WCAG AA contrast.
- No analytics, accounts or network calls at runtime. Static site on GitHub Pages.
- Every player-facing string lives in content, not in components.
- All brand tokens live in `skins/smallbiz/theme.css`.
- Framework: Preact, no state library. Keep motion cheap and respect reduced-motion.

## Aesthetic direction

Editorial and tactile: a well-set report, a rubber stamp, a ledger. Serif display, generous type, hard edges where it counts. Not a SaaS dashboard, not a game-store neon UI, not cards inside cards.
